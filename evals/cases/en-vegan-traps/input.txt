Butternut Pesto Noodles
Serves 2

Ingredients
200 g egg noodles
1 small butternut squash, peeled and diced
2 tbsp olive oil
200 ml coconut milk
3 tbsp basil pesto
1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
20 g butter
30 g Parmesan, grated
Salt and pepper to taste

Method
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C. Toss the squash with the olive oil and roast for 25 minutes.
2. Cook the egg noodles in salted water until tender, then drain.
3. Melt the butter in a large pan, add the coconut milk, pesto and Worcestershire sauce and simmer for 3 minutes.
4. Add the roasted squash and the noodles to the pan and stir to coat.
5. Serve with the Parmesan on top.
